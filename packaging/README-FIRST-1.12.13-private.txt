Pulse Weaver 1.12.13 Beta

Repairs YouTube sign-in on fresh installations by bundling the application's
Google Desktop app registration. Users only click Connect YouTube and sign in
with their own Google account. No developer credential fields are exposed.
Existing local registrations and account settings are retained.

The bundled desktop registration identifies Pulse Weaver, not a user's account.
No personal access tokens, refresh tokens, scenes or settings are distributed.
Google's app verification and account restrictions still apply.

Close Pulse Weaver and previous setup windows, then run this installer.
Do not uninstall first. Full verified app-and-settings backups and rollback
remain enabled. Scenes, transforms, positions and account settings are preserved.
