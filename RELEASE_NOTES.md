# Pulse Weaver 2.0.0-unstable.7 — Camera movement and portrait editing

Animated Looks use smooth camera reflow by default, with coordinated view
handoffs, individual movement and resizing, and gentle acceleration. Framing
changes no longer switch sizing modes on the final animation frame. New Looks
start with a 1.05-second movement; saved custom timings remain available.

The Camera workspace follows how scenes are used: portrait-only helpers appear
in Portrait and shared helpers appear in both formats. Custom-sized camera
scenes display their complete editing area. Scene ownership, source settings,
audio, filters and Stage transitions stay intact.

The selection-first Build my show flow from revision 6 is retained. See
[revision 7](docs/RELEASE-2.0.0-unstable.7.md) for validation and installer details.

## Previous: 2.0.0-unstable.6 — Choose, configure, build

Build my show now has two required pages. Select the layouts and screens you
need, provide only their relevant inputs, then build. Game, screen, face and
activity inputs can be shared across multiple Looks. Starting-only shows need
no camera. Framing/colour, stingers, three alert layers and screen artwork are
optional editors. Original camera filters, audio and monitoring remain intact.

The whole Q3 workflow group informed the coverage review. Activity-camera
arrangements are general; bespoke Yu-Gi-Oh layouts are excluded. Saved layouts
remain available through detailed setup. See [revision 6](docs/RELEASE-2.0.0-unstable.6.md)
for verification boundaries and both installer families.
