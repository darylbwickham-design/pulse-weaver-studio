## Interaction decision

The user rejected six mandatory phases. The revised default has exactly two required pages: select needed outcomes, then supply their inputs and build. Source questions depend on those selections, not an initial activity taxonomy or inputs discovered on the machine. The optional editors for framing/colour, transitions, alerts and screen artwork do not add required pages. The old detailed builder and saved recipe identities remain compatible.

This follows the previously researched task-centred approach: real accomplishments precede internal mechanisms; contextual questions ask for the materials a selected task needs; local correction retains the draft. See [the academic research](SETUP-WIZARD-TASK-FIRST-RESEARCH-2026-10-25.md) and [stream workflow research](SETUP-WIZARD-STREAM-WORKFLOWS-2026-10-25.md). Those sources do not establish a universal optimum of two pages. Two pages is a concrete design response to this user's feedback, to be checked in actual novice use.

## Output constraints

Each selected layout creates one Look. Different inset sizes, corners, fit/crop or colour do not create additional Looks. Game, screen, presenter and activity are distinct role bindings, reused across Looks and formats. A starting-only show has no camera questions. Unselected source drafts are omitted. Games and screens remain uncropped. Camera monochrome uses a native colour-filter wrapper, leaving the original source filters and audio settings intact. Browser alerts remain limited to three independent layers with one copy per used canvas; empty layers create no browser sources.

Automated graph and transaction checks, small-window geometry checks and actual native visual inspection provide technical evidence. They are not an independent first-time-user study or a measured guarantee of coverage for most streamers.
